# Better To-do List (Push Forward Time / P.F.T.)

A planner to-do list that organizes assignments based on **user-configurable Push Forward Time (P.F.T.)** — any time of day the student chooses.

When the feature is enabled, any item due **before** the student’s P.F.T. (local time) appears on the **previous day** as work to do. That way, assignments due early in the morning (e.g. **9:00 AM**) do not only show up on the day they are due for students who plan and complete work the same day.

**Examples:**

- P.F.T. = **9:00 PM** → anything due before 9:00 PM shows up one day earlier on the list.
- P.F.T. = **9:00 AM** → anything due before 9:00 AM that morning is pushed to the prior day’s list.

When disabled, behavior matches today’s Canvas planner bucketing (no surprise changes).
